## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

[err-00006] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not use generic stock‑sale tags (e.g., SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction) for share issuances that are part of an acquisition; instead use the acquisition‑specific tag.
[err-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not treat a cash payment component as the full consideration transferred; use PaymentsToAcquireBusinessesGross for the cash portion and reserve BusinessCombinationConsiderationTransferred1 for the total aggregate amount.
[err-00008] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Do not use SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction for primary share issuances (e.g., shares sold in a primary offering or mandatory convertible preferred stock issuance). Instead, use StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues for new shares created during the period.
## PROBLEM-SOLVING HEURISTICS

[ph-00010] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When the text mentions the company "selling" shares, first determine if the context describes a primary issuance (new shares created) versus a secondary market sale (existing shares transferred). Use StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues for the former and SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction for the latter.
## CONTEXT CLUES & INDICATORS

[ctx-00009] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Key phrases indicating a primary issuance: "primary offering", "sold ... shares of our mandatory convertible preferred stock", "issued new shares", "capital raise", "initial public offering", "shares issued as part of a financing". These suggest the use of StockIssuedDuringPeriodSharesNewIssues.

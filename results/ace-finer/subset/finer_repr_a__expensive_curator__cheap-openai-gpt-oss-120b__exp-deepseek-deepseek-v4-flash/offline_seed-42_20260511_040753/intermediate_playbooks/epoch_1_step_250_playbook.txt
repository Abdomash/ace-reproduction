## PROBLEM-SOLVING HEURISTICS

[ph-00115] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When in doubt between a generic and a specific tag, apply the 'simplest match' heuristic: choose the tag that requires the fewest assumptions about the source text. If the text does not explicitly mention the qualifiers that define the specific tag, use the generic tag. This heuristic helps avoid over-specification errors.
